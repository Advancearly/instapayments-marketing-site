Do I need an existing Stripe account?+

You can connect a Stripe account you already use or create a new one when you set up InstaPayments.

Do I need to write any code?+

No. Use the Checkout Creator to set up a form. Each checkout has a unique link you can put behind a button on your website or share in an email.

What kinds of payments can I accept?+

Create checkouts for one-time payments, subscriptions, and donations. You can also collect a card to save for future use.

What can my customers do in the portal?+

Customers can update their payment methods, manage subscriptions, download or pay invoices, and update their shipping addresses. The branded customer portal is included in the Growth plan.

Is there a transaction fee?+

Yes. All three plans have a 0.5% processing fee on all transactions, in addition to the listed monthly plan price.
